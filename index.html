🍺 What is a “beer mugg”?

A mug with a deep pocket: you drop a cold can inside. It insulates like a koozie, but drinks like a stein — with a proper handle and your name in big letters. No more mystery cans at the braai / BBQ.

🖨️ Print it — real STL included

muse-spark-beer-mugg-holder.stl (4.9 MB, 98k tris) is in this repo: Ø78 × 105 mm mug, Ø68 mm pocket for 66 mm cans, 5 mm walls, 6 mm floor, embossed MUSE SPARK / BEER MUGG 2 mm relief. Slice at 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% gyroid, PETG. Use supports for the handle. Fits Ender-3 bed.
